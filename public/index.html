How it works

Every decision was measured against a local reimplementation of the scorer. The pipeline is shaped around what that metric actually rewards.

retrieval Nearest templates

k training cases ranked by template similarity — within a modality they're near-duplicates, so a template match shows the model exactly how each field of this template transforms.

generation Few-shot draft

An instruction model (Qwen2.5-14B, 4-bit, greedy) gets the house-style guide, the retrieved examples, and this case's template + dictation, and writes a draft report.

post-process Skeleton snap

The draft is forced onto the template's exact skeleton: every label reproduced verbatim, UPPERCASE, in order; unfilled fields fall back to the template sentence.

The RES metric

    
src/res_score.py reimplements the Radiology Edit Score from the
      public spec. Predicting the reference scores exactly 0.0.

    
RES = 0.65·Ffindings + 0.35·Iimpression

    
A field-aware, weighted word-level edit distance after normalization (lowercasing, unit
      spelling, list markers removed, 5mm → 5 mm). FINDINGS aligns
      sections by label.

    

      
Token classWeightExamples

      
        
4.0clinically criticalnegation, laterality, severity, acuity, numbers, mm, cm

        
2.0other contentanatomy, descriptive findings

        
0.25function wordsthe, and, of, with

      
    

    
    
      
  • A field the reference changed from the template weighs 3× an
        untouched one — the score concentrates on the sections the dictation touched.
    • 
      
  • A finding under the wrong label is penalised twice: missing where
        expected, and extra where it isn't.
    • 
      
  • A dropped label scores the maximum for that field.
    • 
      
  • Word order counts — matching the reference's phrasing matters, not
        just its content.

Results & what's next

    
Local 5-fold CV on the challenge scorer, and the public leaderboard.

The residual errors are mostly routing (a finding
      placed in the wrong section) and omissions. Next steps that ran out of
      runway: a verification pass that checks every weight-4 token and every dictated finding is
      present; a QLoRA fine-tune on the training pairs; self-consistency over 3 samples. A
      frontier model in place of the local 14B would likely close much of the gap to the leaders.

    
The pipeline ran end-to-end on a free Kaggle T4 GPU at no cost. It's
      provider-agnostic — src/llm.py also targets a hosted API.
